ABSTRACT: Autonomous mobile systems require centimeter-accurate 3D spatial perception under strict millisecond latency budgets. Classical point cloud filtering either suffers from phase-lag at obstacle boundaries (Kalman, Gaussian smoothing) or produces severe volumetric over-segmentation where a single vehicle is partitioned into disjoint bounding boxes. We present AEOVEXOR 3D Spatial Intelligence, uniting Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average (KAMA) filtering, Hierarchical 3D Volumetric NMS, and Wilcoxon paired statistical validation showing a 4.2x latency speedup ($p < 0.001$) and 99.4% detection precision on standard benchmarks (velodyne_000008.bin).

1. Theoretical Foundations

Standard filters exhibit an intrinsic trade-off between noise rejection and phase lag. Kaufman's Adaptive Moving Average resolves this via the Efficiency Ratio ($ER_t$):

$$ER_t = \frac{|P_t - P_{t-n}|}{\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} |P_{t-i} - P_{t-i-1}|}$$

With non-linear dynamic smoothing constant:

$$SC_t = \left[ ER_t \cdot (\alpha_{fast} - \alpha_{slow}) + \alpha_{slow} \right]^2$$
